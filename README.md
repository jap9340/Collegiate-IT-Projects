# Systems Analysis and Design (SAD)
This was a semester-wide exercise in practical SAD; Our team, over the course of four milestones, iterated through one development cycle given an existing system with a problem for a team of Systems Analysts to solve.
We identified our goal and scope, determined system requirements and gathered information via survey and interview. 
Then, we created Entity Relationship (ER) and Data Flow (DFD) diagrams. 
Finally, we logically modelled and then designed our system, selecting Structured English for modelling and documenting inputs/outputs for design.

Purpose: The purpose of the team project is to apply the systems analysis and design concepts studied in class
and to develop an appreciation for the role of the analyst in the systems development process. The project will:
(1) be governed by a comprehensive development methodology; (2) allow for the use of CASE technology
throughout the life cycle; and (3) provide a practical, first experience in systems analysis and design.
